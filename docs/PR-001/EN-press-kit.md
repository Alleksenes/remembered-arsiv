## Section 3. Press release

**Headline:** Remembered: A living digital archive that carries life stories across generations

**Standfirst:** Photographs, voices, letters, and the stories loved ones tell. Remembered brings the scattered pieces of a life together in one shared story.

**Body:**

A family photograph only means something while someone can still name the faces in it. An old letter only speaks if someone remembers whose hand wrote it. Most of what a life leaves behind gains its meaning through the people who remember.

Remembered begins with that idea: bring personal archives together with the people who can tell their stories. It is being built as a living digital memory space where a person's life story is recorded, enriched by the memories of those close to them, and carried to the generations that come after.

The starting point is simple: every life deserves to be remembered. Remembered meets that approach with a quiet design language that keeps the person, not the platform, at the center.

2. **Who can contribute to a life story?** Family members, friends, colleagues, and anyone who can bear witness; every contribution is attributed, and the verified words of the deceased are never mixed with the testimony of the living. (SEM-20 voice ownership.)
3. **How is content visibility managed?** Visibility is explicit, optional, and reversible; a page follows the choices made while the person was alive, and nothing is shown to anyone unasked. (SEM-24 privacy/legacy.)
